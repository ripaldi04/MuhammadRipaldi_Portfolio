Digital Profile Masuda Rukost IPB

masudarukostipb.com is a digital profile platform for Masuda Rukost, an exclusive boarding house strategically located in the Institut Pertanian Bogor area. This website is designed to provide convenience for students or parents to review facilities, room availability, and living environment virtually before conducting an on-site survey.

Main Features:

  • Room Showcase: Details specifications of each room type (size, furniture, and in-room facilities).
  • Facility Highlights: Visual gallery displaying supporting facilities such as parking area, WiFi, 24-hour security, and communal spaces.
  • Strategic Location Map: Highlighting the accessibility of housing near campus gates and public facilities.
  • One-Click Inquiry: Direct integration with WhatsApp service to facilitate prospective tenants in checking room availability in real-time.
  • House Rules & Info: Transparent information regarding boarding house rules and rental fees.

Technology Used:

  • Platform: WordPress CMS for independent and fast content management.
  • Design Framework: Lightweight and mobile-friendly theme customization, considering the student target audience that predominantly uses smartphones.
  • Local SEO Optimization: Keyword optimization techniques to make the website easily found on Google Maps and local searches around IPB.
  • Lead Conversion Tools: Use of chat widgets and responsive contact forms.

What I Did:

  • Structured website information architecture so key information (price, location, facilities) is easily found by users.
  • Performed curation and optimization of visual assets (room and building photos) to keep website loading fast.
  • Integrated single-page navigation system for a simpler user experience.
  • Set up automated messaging to the boarding house management team when a prospective tenant inquires via the website.
  • Ensured display compatibility across various device screen sizes (Responsive Design).

Through the presence of this website, Masuda Rukost IPB has successfully increased marketing efficiency and given a professional impression as a modern and trusted residence for IPB academic community.
