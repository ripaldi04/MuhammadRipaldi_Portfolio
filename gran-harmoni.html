Company Profile Website for Strategic & Comfortable Housing in Bekasi.

Digital Presence of Gran Harmoni Cibitung

granharmonicibitung.com is the official website profile for the Gran Harmoni Cibitung housing project. This website functions as an interactive digital brochure that presents comprehensive information about subsidized and commercial housing, environmental facilities, and construction progress for prospective buyers.

Website Features:

  • Product Showcase: Catalog of house types with building specifications and floor plans details.
  • Virtual Tour & Gallery: Visual documentation of sample units (show unit) and housing area facilities.
  • Location & Accessibility: Map integration and information regarding distance to transportation facilities and toll gates.
  • Direct Sales Integration: Quick access button to WhatsApp Marketing to facilitate reservations and inquiries.

Technology Used:

  • Platform: WordPress CMS for flexible content management.
  • Frontend: Custom responsive layout using Elementor/page builder for an elegant display on mobile screens.
  • Optimization: On-Page SEO integration to target property searches in Cibitung and Bekasi area.

What I Did:

  • Designed website information architecture to focus on prospective buyer conversion.
  • Performed visual design customization that is clean and modern according to Gran Harmoni branding.
  • Optimized house image assets so pages load quickly without reducing visual quality.
  • Set up navigation system that makes it easy for users to find unit types according to their budget.
  • Ensured *call-to-action* (CTA) integration is spread effectively across each main page.
