Taman Alhambra Landing Page

This project is the development of a Conversion-Focused Landing Page for Taman Alhambra. Unlike a complex corporate website, this page is designed linearly to guide prospective buyers to understand the housing *value proposition*, view the unit gallery, and take action (Call to Action) all on one intuitive page.

Landing Page Structure & Features:

  • Hero Section with Impact: Main visual highlighting housing aesthetics with a persuasive main message (Headline).
  • Unique Selling Points (USP): Concise information about location advantages, Islamic concept, and area facilities in iconic form.
  • Product Showcase Gallery: Photo slider gallery of units and floor plans optimized to remain lightweight when accessed via mobile.
  • One-Tap Contact (CTA): Integration of floating WhatsApp button and quick form to facilitate instant prospect conversion.
  • Location & Map Integration: Interactive Google Maps section to provide an accurate view of the housing's strategic position.

Technology Used:

  • Platform: WordPress with Page Builder for design layout flexibility.
  • Speed Optimization: Implementation of *Lazy Loading* on high-resolution images to maintain access speed.
  • Mobile First Design: Full responsiveness considering the majority of ad traffic (Ads) comes from smartphone users.
  • Analytics Integration: Basic setup for visitor tracking for marketing effectiveness evaluation needs.

What I Did:

  • Arranged the layout (layout) based on *Customer Journey* so the information flow flows logically from top to bottom.
  • Performed visual asset compression without reducing quality for maximum *loading speed* performance.
  • Integrated the "Direct-to-WhatsApp" system to facilitate prospective consumers consulting directly.
  • Ensured the design remains clean (clean) and professional in accordance with the exclusive housing image.
  • Set up reliable Domain and Hosting to handle traffic spikes during ad campaigns.
