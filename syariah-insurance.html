Sharia General Insurance Information Portal

asuransikerugiansyariah.com is a company profile website dedicated to educating and providing information about general protection products based on mutual assistance (Takaful) principles. This website serves as a bridge between insurance companies and customers who need asset protection solutions that align with sharia values in Indonesia.

Main Website Features:

  • Sharia Governance Info: Detailed explanation of the mechanism for managing tabarru' funds and justice principles in sharia insurance.
  • Product Directory: Comprehensive catalog for various protections, ranging from motor vehicles, property, to marine cargo insurance.
  • Claim Guide Center: Step-by-step guide to transparent claim procedures to facilitate customers during emergencies.
  • Download Center: Quick access to download standard policies, claim forms, and annual company reports.
  • Integrated Contact Point: Form system directly connected to the admin team for quotation premium requests.

Technology Used:

  • Platform: WordPress CMS with advanced customization for optimal performance.
  • Framework: Responsive Design Framework to ensure accessibility on all devices (Desktop, Tablet, Mobile).
  • Advanced Security: Implementation of high-level SSL/TLS and security hardening to protect visitor data.
  • Optimization: Technical SEO focusing on financial industry keywords and local insurance services.

What I Did:

  • Designed a clean and elegant user interface (UI) to build trust with prospective customers.
  • Structured informative content about the fundamental differences between conventional and sharia insurance.
  • Optimized page load speed (Page Speed) to ensure user comfort when searching for information.
  • Integrated branch office location map feature to facilitate access to in-person services.
  • Performed email marketing integration configuration for managing customer lead database.

This website has successfully positioned Sharia General Insurance as a credible digital protection service provider, while also serving as an effective sharia financial literacy tool for the general public.
