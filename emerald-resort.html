Website Company Profile The Emeralda Resort Bandung

theemeraldaresortbandung.com is a company profile platform for exclusive resort housing located in Padalarang, West Bandung Regency. This website is designed to highlight the modern housing aesthetic that blends with nature, while also providing in-depth information about unit types, facilities, and investment advantages in that area.

Main Features:

  • Visual Showcase: Photo gallery to display the beauty of architecture and resort environment.
  • Unit Type Information: Complete details about building specifications, layout, and advantages of each unit type.
  • Interactive Map & Location: Strategic location information and accessibility to public facilities around the resort.
  • Lead Generation: Integration of WhatsApp button and contact form to facilitate potential buyers connecting with the sales team.
  • News & Event Updates: Special section to inform about construction progress and community events at the resort.

Technology Used:

  • Platform: WordPress CMS for dynamic and flexible content management.
  • Page Builder: Elementor/Custom Builder to ensure luxurious and responsive design.
  • Image Optimization: Visual asset compression technique to keep website loading fast even with many high-resolution photos.
  • On-Page SEO: Local keyword optimization to improve ranking in Bandung property search results.

What I Did:

  • Built website navigation structure that makes it easy for users to find unit and price information.
  • Performed design customization to match the elegant and exclusive brand identity of The Emeralda.
  • Optimized website performance on mobile devices (Smartphone) for easy access by potential buyers anytime.
  • Integrated direct communication tool (Direct Chat) to speed up the lead conversion process.
  • Performed server and domain configuration to ensure the website has optimal stability and security.

Through this website, The Emeralda Resort Bandung has successfully strengthened its digital image as one of the most prestigious resort residences in the West Bandung area, while also becoming the main marketing tool for the sales team.
